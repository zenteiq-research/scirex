# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The summary below is the working version for this repository; where it is silent,
the Covenant applies.

## Our pledge

We want participation in ScireX to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation, and whether they come from academia or industry.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, including with first-time contributors.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's ideas, code and data.
- Keep discussion on the technical or scientific question at hand.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information (an address, a personal e-mail, a
  private chat) without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
mailing lists) and when someone represents the project in public.

Report a problem by e-mail to [**research@zenteiq.ai**](mailto:research@zenteiq.ai). Do not use a public issue.
Reports are handled in confidence by the maintainers
(see the [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md)). A maintainer who is involved in the report
does not take part in handling it.

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, close or lock threads, and ask a person to
stop. For repeated or serious violations they may ban a person from the project
spaces, following the enforcement ladder in the Contributor Covenant (correction,
warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
