## How to sign

- **As an individual:** comment the exact sentence below on your first pull request. The bot
  records your GitHub account, the date and the version of this agreement, and then lets the
  pull request be merged. You sign once; it covers all your later contributions.

  > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

- **On behalf of a company:** if your employer owns, or may own, what you write, an authorised
  person of the company must sign the [corporate agreement](CLA-CORPORATE.md) before your pull
  request is merged. Write to [**research@zenteiq.ai**](mailto:research@zenteiq.ai), name the company and the people it covers,
  and return the signed agreement.

ZenteiQ employees and contractors whose agreements already assign their work to ZenteiQ, and
automated accounts such as dependency bots, do not sign.
